What are the problems in construction?

The usual ones are delays, budget overruns and unclear plans. We agree the scope, the schedule and a fixed price before work starts, and report progress every week.

What is best practice in construction?

Plan carefully, use quality materials, follow safety rules and keep clear communication with the client from start to finish.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on the size. Small renovations take about two weeks, while full home builds can take eight weeks or more.
